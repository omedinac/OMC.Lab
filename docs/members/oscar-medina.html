Oscar Medina

ImportantPosition

Medical Sciences Researcher at Hospital Infantil de México, a National Health Institute

I am the head of the Immunometabolism laboratory in the Epidemiology, Endocrinology & Nutrition research unit and a teaching professor at Universidad Anáhuac México (AUMxN).

My research primarily focuses on how intestinal immune cells regulate experimental models of Crohn’s disease and ulcerative colitis, and has been supported by prestigious organizations such as the National Council for Science and Technology and the Crohn’s and Colitis Foundation of America.

My journey in science began at the State of Mexico Autonomous University, where I earned my bachelor’s degree in Biology. I later completed my Ph.D. in Molecular Biomedicine at the Center for Research and Advanced Studies (CINVESTAV) under the guidance of Vianney Ortiz-Navarrete, followed by postdoctoral training at Emory University (Departments of Pediatrics and Pathology & Laboratory Medicine) under the mentorship of Tim Denning and Charles Parkos.

In addition to my research and teaching, I have served on the editorial boards of Gene Reports and Mediators of Inflammation. I am a proud member of several esteemed scientific communities, including The Mexican Academy of Sciences, The National Research System (SNII), The Society for Mucosal Immunology, The Mexican Society for Immunology, The American Association of Immunologists, and The American Society for Investigative Pathology.
