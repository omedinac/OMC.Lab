Clément Fontan

Position: Professor at UcLouvain

I am a Professor of European Economic Policy at UcLouvain since 2018 and I was the co-editor of the journal Politique Européenne between 2018 and 2024. My research focuses on critical political economy, European economic governance, financial ethics, central banking and the politics of sustainable finance.
